DOCUMENTS TO BE UPLOADED (Specimen Scholarship Portal 2026-27)

1. Recent passport size photograph with white background, size 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm,
   in JPG/JPEG format, file size between 20 KB and 50 KB.
2. Scanned signature in JPG format, file size 10 KB to 20 KB.
3. Class 12 marksheet in PDF format, maximum 200 KB. Upload front and back in a single file.
4. Income certificate issued on or after 01-04-2026, PDF only, not more than 0.3 MB.
5. First page of bank passbook showing account holder name and IFSC, PDF, up to 1 MB.
6. Caste certificate (if applicable), PDF, maximum 300 KB.

Enter your Aadhaar number in the form; do not upload the Aadhaar card.
File names must be in the format RollNumber_DocumentName.
